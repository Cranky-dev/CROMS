Copyright & DMCA Policy

CROMS respects the intellectual property rights of others. However, CROMS does not host the archived media retrieved through the software. We are in no way affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the archival projects we utilize.

Copyright concerns regarding material hosted by an external archival project should be directed to that project's operators through their official channels.

Complaints concerning archive content

If you believe material accessible through CROMS infringes your copyright, please provide us with the identity of the copyrighted work in question.

Using that information, we will identify the relevant archival project hosting the material and direct you to its official contact channels.

CROMS does not host or control the archived material and cannot remove content from third-party archive servers.

Copyright complaints and removal requests must ultimately be submitted to the operators of the archive hosting the material. CROMS does not adjudicate copyright claims or independently determine whether material is infringing.

Copyright complaints should include the following information:

  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right allegedly infringed.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may not contain sufficient information for us to identify or address the alleged infringement.
